# License and External Asset Policy

ReproForge separates the license of this framework from the licenses of research papers and external assets.

## Mandatory separation

Treat these as independent licensing surfaces:

1. paper text and figures,
2. author or third-party source code,
3. datasets,
4. models and model weights,
5. benchmark assets and APIs,
6. ReproForge's own code.

Permission in one category does not imply permission in another.

## Default rules

- Cite the original paper and canonical source.
- Do not redistribute full paper PDFs, copied prose, or figures unless permission clearly allows it.
- Do not copy source code unless its license permits the intended use and all obligations are satisfied.
- Do not redistribute datasets or model weights merely because they are publicly downloadable.
- Record `UNKNOWN` when rights cannot be established. Never convert uncertainty into assumed permission.
- Prefer download instructions or source references over bundling third-party assets.
- Preserve required notices and attribution for compatible third-party code.

## Independent reproduction

An `INDEPENDENT` implementation should be written from the research description and should not copy protected source code. Reading public code for comparison can affect the defensibility of an independence claim; studies should disclose relevant exposure when material.

## Patents, terms, privacy, and other restrictions

Copyright and open-source licenses do not resolve patent rights, API/service terms, privacy obligations, export controls, database rights, or other legal restrictions. ReproForge does not make those determinations automatically.

This policy is a research governance rule, not legal advice.
